3. Bubble sort is easier to understand because it follows a very straightforward process: repeatedly compare two adjacent elements and swap them if they are in the wrong order. This makes the logic simple and easy to visualize. Insertion sort is slightly more complex because it involves shifting elements and keeping track of where to insert the current value. While it is still not very difficult, it requires a better understanding of how the sorted portion of the list is being built. So overall, bubble sort is easier to understand, but insertion sort is more efficient.
